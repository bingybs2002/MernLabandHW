# Comprehensive

### 1. What does your day to day life look like?

I usually start by checking messages, tickets, PR comments, and any urgent issues, then attend the Daily Scrum. Most of my day is spent implementing and testing features, debugging issues, reviewing PRs, and collaborating with teammates, the PO, and QA. I also update Jira and communicate blockers or risks. Near the end of the day, I make sure my work is pushed, documented appropriately, and that the team knows my current status.
